What the program is

Bloom Mental Health sends customer-care text messages to people who ask for them: confirmations of appointment requests, appointment reminders, links to join a telehealth visit, and replies to questions you text us. We never send marketing or promotional texts, and texts never include diagnoses, medications, or other clinical details.

How to join

Joining is optional. On our appointment request form, enter your mobile number and check the box next to this statement:

Optional: By providing your phone number and checking this box, you agree to receive customer-care text messages from Bloom Mental Health about your request and your appointments, such as confirmations and reminders. Message frequency may vary. Standard Message and Data Rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. We will not share mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. See our Text Message Program, SMS Terms, and Privacy Policy.

The box is unchecked unless you check it, the form works without it, and the phone number is optional. We only text a number whose owner agreed on our form; we never text a number someone else gave us.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency may vary, usually a few texts around each request or appointment. Standard Message and Data Rates may apply.

Stop or get help

Reply STOP to any text to opt out. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, call 702-350-1419, or email michelle@bloommentalhealthlv.com. To join again later, reply START or check the box on the form again.

Privacy

We will not share mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Details are in our Text Message Program, SMS Terms, and Privacy Policy.

Request an appointment, with or without texts

A person from our team calls, emails or texts you back to set a time. Texts are only sent if you check the box.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 or call 911. This form is not monitored around the clock.

Our goal is to create a safe and welcoming space where individuals feel heard, understood, and empowered throughout their journey to better mental health.

In crisis? Call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or call 911. Bloom is an outpatient practice, not an emergency service.
