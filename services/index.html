Services

A full continuum of outpatient psychiatric care, tailored to you

From a first evaluation to long-term medication management, therapy and intensive outpatient support, in the office or by telehealth.

Services & Clinical Support

  • Psychiatric Evaluations

    Comprehensive assessment to clarify symptoms, diagnoses, treatment needs, and next steps.

  • Medication Management

    Ongoing evaluation of medication effectiveness, tolerability, adherence, and treatment goals.

  • ADHD Evaluation & Treatment

    Assessment and treatment planning for attention, executive-function, and related concerns.

  • Anxiety & Depression Treatment

    Evidence-informed psychiatric care for common mood and anxiety concerns.

  • Bipolar Disorder Treatment

    Longitudinal medication and symptom management with attention to mood stability.

  • PTSD & Trauma Treatment

    Psychiatric support for trauma-related symptoms and associated mental health concerns.

  • Child & Adolescent Mental Health

    Developmentally informed psychiatric assessment and treatment for younger patients.

  • Autism

    Psychiatric support for autism-spectrum concerns alongside co-occurring mental health needs.

  • Psychotherapy

    Individual talk therapy offered alongside medication management for comprehensive care.

  • Substance Use & MAT

    Medication-assisted treatment and psychiatric support for substance use concerns.

  • IOP Services

    Intensive outpatient programming for patients who benefit from a higher level of structured support.

  • Telehealth Services

    Secure, confidential video visits from the comfort of home.
